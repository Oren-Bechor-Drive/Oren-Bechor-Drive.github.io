# Stopping and parking sources - 2026-11-05

The owner asked for a standalone stopping and parking topic on 2026-11-05, after two supplied lesson clips showed illegal stopping by a public-transport curb near a school. This review supports the `course/parking/` reading page and its public practice quiz. It is a source check, not a legal certification or instructor approval.

## Sources and access

- The [official theory question bank](https://data.gov.il/api/3/action/datastore_search?resource_id=bf7cb748-f220-474b-a4d5-2d59f93db28d&limit=10000) was downloaded directly on 2026-11-05. The response SHA-256 matched the `responseSha256` recorded in [the quiz source](theory-quiz-content.json), so these questions come from the same snapshot as the existing quizzes. The same normalization reproduced all 129 existing non-adapted entries exactly.
- The [sign chart](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/policy/tamrurim_7924_01_18/he/1694327856_%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%9D_%D7%9E%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%9C%D7%91_0922.pdf) (September 2022 edition) was downloaded directly and read for markings 511, 817 and 818 and the black-and-white curb marking 816.
- Five question images were downloaded from the bank's gov.il image URLs and added to [the media map](theory-media.json).
- No consolidated official text of the Traffic Regulations was obtained. Distances below come from bank answers, which show what the examination teaches; they are not a complete statement of the regulations.

## Claims used in the lesson

| Claim | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Parking means leaving a vehicle for any time other than immediately picking up or dropping off people or loading or unloading goods without interruption. | Bank question 0012, correct answer. |
| Red-white curbs: stopping and parking on the adjacent roadway are prohibited. | Sign chart marking 818. |
| Red-yellow curbs: stopping is prohibited except for the vehicle type a sign permits, only to pick up and drop off passengers. | Sign chart marking 511; bank question 0609. |
| Blue-white curbs: paid parking according to the posted sign. | Sign chart marking 817; bank question 0657 describes it as parking under the local authority's arrangement. |
| Black-white curbs highlight sidewalks, dividers and built traffic islands. | Sign chart marking 816. |
| Parking in a bus stop is allowed only as the stop's sign states; where weekend and holiday parking is posted, it is for private vehicles only. | Bank question 1403. |
| No standing within 12 m of a junction unless a sign or marking permits it. | Bank question 0203. |
| No stopping, parking or standing on a crosswalk or within 12 m before it; standing after it is allowed. | Bank questions 1766 and 0206. |
| No standing from 20 m before a bus-stop sign to 20 m after it unless marked otherwise; opposite a stop only on a roadway at least 12 m wide. | Bank questions 0212 and 0213. |
| No standing within 2 m of a fire hydrant. | Bank question 0204. |
| No standing beside a pedestrian safety railing unless a sign permits it. | Bank question 0214. |
| No standing beside a vehicle parked at the roadside, or on a bridge or in a tunnel. | Bank questions 0210 and 0211. |
| No stopping on an expressway except on the shoulder after a breakdown that prevents continuing. | Bank question 1398. |
| Park parallel to the roadway edge or as marked; angled parking only where angled marking exists; wheels within 40 cm of the curb. | Bank questions 0221, 0222, 0223 and 1553. |
| Check before getting out that leaving the vehicle does not disturb or endanger road users. | Bank question 0951. |
| The registered owner is responsible for a vehicle parked illegally unless they prove they were not the driver and identify the driver. | Bank question 1736. |

## Excluded or qualified

- Bank question 0658 says red-white curbs allow taxis to stop for passengers. The 2022 sign chart's text for marking 818 does not mention that exception; separate bank questions (1171, 1172, 1227) describe taxi permissions. Because the course teaches private-car drivers, the lesson states the marking as the sign chart does, and 0658 was not selected.
- Bank question 0044 repeats question 0951, which is already in the driving-test quiz, and was not selected. Questions 0221, 0223 and 0951 stay in the driving-test quiz. They now link to the parking explanation.
- Heavy-vehicle, bus, taxi, LPG and lighting-time parking questions were not selected because the course targets private-car learners.

## Lesson media

The owner decided on 2026-11-05 that this topic uses drawings instead of video: the supplied clips of stopping at a bus-stop curb were not realistic teaching material. The parking section shows step-by-step drawings of parallel, perpendicular and angle parking in `assets/images/parking/`. The first section shows each curb color beside its explanation, and the second has a street map of the no-parking stretches with their distances. The drawings were made for the course and are not official diagrams. The steering sequences are standard technique and need Oren's review like the rest of the topic.
