# Israeli lesson sources - 2026-09-25

Publication follow-up: the owner subsequently authorized implementing variable-length theory quizzes. See [the published selection and review](theory-quiz-review-2026-09-25.md). The initial research below is retained as provenance.

This review supports filling the definitions, narrow-road priority and new-driver reading sections. It uses government teaching material and current Ministry service information. It is a source check, not a legal certification or instructor approval. No quiz questions were selected in this review.

## Access and evidence limits

- Direct web requests to the Ministry's accompaniment service and the July 2024, December 2024 and February 2026 instructor examination PDFs returned HTTP 403. A separate Python request to all three PDFs returned the same result. The findings below were read from indexed first-party content exposed by web search, not from a successful direct PDF download.
- Government examination answers are useful evidence of the rules being taught at the examination date. They are not a consolidated statute or proof that every subsequent amendment has been incorporated. Indexed PDF text also contains extraction errors, so use careful paraphrases rather than quoting corrupted text.
- No current consolidated official text of the entire Traffic Regulations was obtained. Do not describe this review as a complete verification of Israeli traffic law as of the review date.
- An old Knesset traffic-law proposal surfaced in search. It is a proposal and was not used as evidence of enacted law. A military traffic-order text for Judea and Samaria was also excluded as a source for national definitions.

## Definitions

| Claim suitable for the lesson | First-party evidence and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| A way, `דרך`, includes places where the public has a right of passage, including streets, alleys, bridges and open places. It is broader than the vehicle roadway. | The [State Comptroller's 2023 local-road maintenance report](https://www.mevaker.gov.il/sites/DigitalLibrary/Documents/2023/2023-Shilton/2023-Shilton-303-Drachim.pdf), normative-background section, reproduces Traffic Ordinance section 1. |
| A roadway, `כביש`, is the part intended, constructed or improved for vehicle movement, or customarily used by vehicles. It excludes the shoulder. | The [official road-safety textbook](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/reports/driving_textbook/he/publications_2017_nohagim_aheret_nohagim_nachon.pdf), printed page 37, explains the roadway definition. Use this stable definition only; its adjacent shoulder-width text is outdated. |
| A lane, `נתיב`, may be marked or unmarked. Its width accommodates one column of vehicles, with the definition excluding two-wheeled vehicles. | [July 2024 driving-instructor law answers](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/dynamiccollectorresultitem/drivingteacher-07-24-type2/he/final-exams_drivingteacher-07-24-type2-answers.pdf), answer 7. Do not teach that absent paint means absent lanes. |
| The shoulder, `שול הדרך`, is the area next to the roadway edge without an adjacent sidewalk, up to 4 metres or the nearer drainage-channel edge. | July 2024 answer 7 above. The [Road Safety Authority's shoulder page](https://www.gov.il/ar/pages/road_shoulders) independently uses 4 metres. Do not copy the textbook's older 3-metre value. |
| A junction, `צומת`, is the meeting area of two or more roadways, bounded by their edges or their imaginary continuations. A driveway, parking entrance or petrol-station access is not automatically a junction. | July 2024 answer 7 and the [June 2025 instructor methodology answers](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/dynamiccollectorresultitem/drivingteacher-06-25-type3/he/final-exams_drivingteacher-06-25-type3-answers.pdf), printed page 18. The definition excludes motorway merges, the listed property-access connections unless signed otherwise, and the specified signed merge on a one-way roadway with two or more lanes. A short learner definition should acknowledge exceptions. |
| Giving priority means allowing the entitled road user to continue without being forced to stop, wait, change speed or change course. Merely slowing one's own vehicle does not establish that priority was given. | [February 2025 instructor methodology answers](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/dynamiccollectorresultitem/drivingteacher-02-25-type3/he/final-exams_drivingteacher-02-25-type3-answers.pdf), answer 6, definition and explanation. |

## Meeting on a narrow, steep road

The [December 2024 instructor law answers](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/dynamiccollectorresultitem/drivingteacher-12-24-type2/he/final-exams_drivingteacher-12-24-type2-answers.pdf), answer 1, support these bounded additions:

- If opposing vehicles cannot pass freely on a narrow roadway, both drivers slow and move right as far as necessary and safe to avoid collision.
- On a narrow, steep road where the vehicles cannot pass together, the descending driver gives priority to the ascending vehicle. The textbook's section on narrow mountain roads states the stopping condition explicitly: when the situation requires one vehicle to stop, the descending vehicle stops.
- This is not a rule granting ascending traffic priority at every junction or on every slope. Follow the applicable traffic control and signs, including the sign requiring priority for opposing traffic at a narrow section.
- A blocked lane has its own priority rule; do not collapse every obstruction and slope scenario into the same shortcut.

These are the teaching rules corresponding to the requested regulation 46 topic. This review did not obtain a directly readable official consolidated regulation 46, so a learner source label should name the official teaching source rather than imply that the linked document is the regulations themselves.

## New drivers and accompaniment

The [February 2026 instructor law answers](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/dynamiccollectorresultitem/drivingteacher-02-26-type2/he/final-exams_drivingteacher-02-26-type2-answers.pdf), answer 5, support the following distinctions:

- The basic new-driver period is two years; it can be extended. Holding only a motorcycle, tractor or mobile-machine licence previously does not remove new-driver status when the relevant first licence is obtained.
- A young new driver is a new driver under 24. Night accompaniment means 21:00-06:00. The accompanying driver sits in the seat beside the driver.
- After mandatory accompaniment, a new driver under 21 may carry at most two passengers without a qualifying companion beside them. This is not a restriction on every driver under 21 regardless of new-driver status.
- Special offences can affect renewal and extend status. Avoid adding a fixed exhaustive offence list or personal renewal decision without checking the applicable current procedure.

The [Ministry's accompaniment declaration service](https://www.gov.il/he/service/new_driver_accompaniment_period_statement) supplies the practical programme:

- Six months: three months with a companion at all hours, then three months at night. New drivers aged 24 or older are exempt from accompaniment.
- At least 50 accompanied hours: 20 urban, 15 nonurban and 15 at night.
- The ordinary companion routes are age 24 or older with five years of same-class driving entitlement, or age 30 or older with three years. The licence must be valid. Driving-instructor qualification provides an age exception. Do not present an intoxicated person as a qualifying companion.
- Submit the completion declaration; elapsed time alone does not complete the administrative requirement. Check the permit's actual validity, especially around age 24 or an additional permit.

The service's passenger sentence omits the new-driver qualification. Prefer the more precise examination wording above. These additions should be introduced as the ordinary private-car case; exemptions and personal licensing decisions remain with the Ministry.

## Course-summary mapping

The February 2026 law answers, answer 2, support checking traffic before changing lanes, yielding when one's lane ends and signalling before a manoeuvre. The official road-safety textbook's junction discussion supports checking that the junction can be cleared before entering, even on green. A short course summary may paraphrase those principles and link back to the existing detailed lessons. Reflection prompts about observations and instructor feedback are teaching guidance, not additional legal rules.

## Wording review after drafting

The draft review added the junction exceptions, explicit stopping/waiting in the priority definition, and the descending driver's obligation to stop when one vehicle must stop. Accompaniment starts at permit issuance. Prior motorcycle/tractor/mobile-machine entitlement does not remove the ordinary new-driver status.

The new-driver sign remains displayed at the lower rear window while a private-car driver has new-driver status. The official textbook and [October 2024 licensing report, section 10.2](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/reports/report-license220824/he/report-drive301024.pdf) support this addition. The [renewal service](https://www.gov.il/he/service/driving_license_renewal) and [duplicate service](https://www.gov.il/he/service/duplicate_drivers_license_in_case_of_loss) support the final licensing paragraph.

The reasonable-speed definition uses the [December 2024 instructor driving-method answers](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/dynamiccollectorresultitem/drivingteacher-12-24-type/he/final-exams_drivingteacher-12-24-type-answers.pdf). It distinguishes the maximum permitted speed from the speed appropriate to conditions and vehicle control.

The [Ministry of Education junction chapter](https://meyda.education.gov.il/files/Curriculum/high-school/download/traffic-s15-junction.pdf) also specifies the signed one-way merge exception used in the short definition.
