# Driving Learning

This context describes the public learning experience that introduces Oren Bachor's driving course and connects road rules with situations encountered while driving.

## Language

**Learner**:
A visitor preparing to drive or improving their understanding of correct driving.
_Avoid_: Customer, student account

**Learning topic**:
A named area of driving knowledge, such as right-of-way, lane choice, intersections, or trip planning.
_Avoid_: Feature, category

**Topic preview**:
The welcome-page experience where a learner selects a learning topic and reads what it teaches.
_Avoid_: Course dashboard, lesson player

**Road media**:
A visual that illustrates a driving concept or a road environment, including road photographs, road-sign illustrations, and car artwork.
_Avoid_: Decoration, generic stock graphic

**Instructor introduction**:
The welcome-page introduction to Oren Bachor, his teaching approach, and his experience with students.
_Avoid_: Instructor profile account

**Student photo**:
A supplied photograph of Oren with students after passing a driving test.
_Avoid_: Testimonial, pass-rate evidence

**Student gallery**:
The collection of student photos presented within the instructor introduction.
_Avoid_: Lesson gallery, course dashboard

**Welcome direction**:
A distinct way to introduce the course to a new learner, such as the Learning Journey, Instructor-led, or Course-dashboard direction.
_Avoid_: Theme, variant
